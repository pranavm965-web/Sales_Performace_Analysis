# 📊 Superstore Sales Performance Analysis

Welcome to the **Sales Performance Analysis** repository! This project evaluates enterprise sales data across geographic regions, product categories, and time periods to uncover growth drivers and revenue trends.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview & Goals
* **Goal:** Analyze sales performance across regions and categories to identify best/worst performing products and seasonality.
* **Dataset:** `superstore_final_dataset (1).csv`

---

## 🛠️ Key Steps & Methodology
1. **Data Cleaning & Normalization:** Standardized date formats, handled missing postal codes, and removed duplicates.
2. **KPI Computation:** Calculated core business metrics including Total Revenue, Total Profit, Overall Profit Margin, and Average Order Value (AOV).
3. **Time-Series Analysis:** Tracked monthly sales trends to highlight seasonal spikes and performance fluctuations.
4. **Regional & Category Breakdown:** Evaluated performance by geographic region and product sub-categories.

---

## 📈 Key Recommendations for Alfido Tech
1. **Regional Logistical Focus:** Shift inventory and warehousing priority toward top-performing regions.
2. **High-Margin Product Push:** Boost marketing for high-revenue sub-categories (like Phones and Chairs).
3. **Seasonal Campaign Planning:** Schedule automated promotional campaigns ahead of peak sales months.
4. **Cart Basket Optimization:** Introduce cross-category bundling to increase Average Order Value (AOV).
5. **Segment-Specific Loyalty:** Tailor reward incentives based on customer segment types (Consumer vs. Corporate).
